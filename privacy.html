Privacy and Terms

EDU LIBRARY MW · Last updated 17 October 2026

In short: We do not ask for your real name, email or phone number. We keep only what the app needs to work. You can delete your account and your data yourself, any time.

1. What we collect

  • Your username and a 6-digit PIN. The PIN is stored in a scrambled (hashed) form, so nobody can read it.
  • A recovery code shown once when you sign up, in case you forget your PIN.
  • Your chats with Edu AI, if you use the AI helper. They are saved on your phone.
  • A device ID, a random number saved on your phone. It helps us stop misuse and spam.
  • Your internet address (IP), used only for short-term limits that protect the app from abuse.

We do not collect your real name, email, phone number, contacts, location or fingerprint. We do not show ads and we do not sell your information.

2. How we use it

  • To sign you in to your account.
  • To answer your questions in Edu AI.
  • To keep the app safe, fair and working for everyone.

3. Edu AI and other services

When you ask Edu AI a question, your message (and any photo you send) is passed to outside AI services so they can write an answer. These include Mistral and Google Gemini. Picture creation uses pollinations.ai. Your account data is stored with our hosting providers (such as Supabase and Vercel).

Please do not type or photograph private things such as passwords, home addresses or phone numbers in Edu AI.

4. Data on your phone

Downloaded books, your settings, your chats and your device ID are saved on your phone, not in cookies used for tracking. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Clearing the app data removes these.

5. How long we keep it

  • Chats: kept on your phone until you delete them or clear the app data.
  • Your account (username, scrambled PIN, recovery code): kept until you delete your account.
  • Safety limits (IP and device checks): kept only for a short time.

6. Your choices

  • Use Delete my chats to remove your chat history.
  • Use Delete my account and data to remove everything linked to your username.
  • If you are a child, ask a parent, guardian or teacher to read this page with you.

7. Children

Edu Library is made for pupils and students of all ages, including children. That is why we ask for so little. Parents and guardians are welcome to contact us with questions or to ask us to remove a child's account.

8. Rules for using the app

  • Use the app for learning. Do not misuse it, attack it, or try to get into other people's accounts.
  • Do not share your PIN or recovery code.
  • Do not use Edu AI to write hateful, harmful or inappropriate content.
  • We may stop an account that breaks these rules.

9. Books, exams and AI answers

  • Books and past papers belong to their owners. They are shared here for education. If you own something here and want it removed, contact us.
  • Edu AI and the AI-marked exams can make mistakes. Check important answers with your teacher or textbook. Use the report button if an answer looks wrong.
  • We try to keep the app running, but we cannot promise it will always be available.

10. Changes

If we change this page, the date at the top will change. If the change is important, we will tell you in the app.
